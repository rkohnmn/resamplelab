## 1. Architectural Overview & Separation of Concerns

ResampleLab follows the canonical DataSense Studio zero-dependency, 100% client-side modular architecture specified in `APPLICATION_GUIDELINES.md`. All mathematical, probabilistic, and resampling calculations are strictly decoupled from DOM manipulation and HTML5 Canvas rendering.

## 2. Separation of Statistical Distribution Objects (Part 6.4)

To prevent the conceptual conflation of distinct statistical distributions, ResampleLab represents and labels each distribution with explicit metadata (`distributionObject.objectType`, `conditionalOn`, `centeredAt`):
1. **Population Distribution ($F$)**: Theoretical probability model or finite universe $\Omega$ centered at parameter $\theta$ with spread $\sigma$.
2. **Empirical Sample Distribution ($\hat{F}_n$)**: Observed sample $S = \{x_1, \dots, x_n\}$ placing mass $1/n$ on each observation, centered at $\hat{\theta}$ with spread $s$.
3. **Sampling Distribution**: Distribution of $\hat{\theta} = T(S)$ across repeated fresh samples from $F$, centered at $\theta$ (up to estimator bias) with spread $SE(\hat{\theta})$.
4. **Bootstrap Distribution**: Distribution of $\hat{\theta}^{*b}$ across resamples drawn WITH replacement from $\hat{F}_n$, conditional on $S$ and centered at $\hat{\theta}_{\text{obs}}$ with spread $\widehat{SE}_{\text{boot}}$.
5. **Null Randomization / Permutation Distribution**: Distribution of $T^{*b}$ generated by permuting labels or flipping signs WITHOUT replacement under $H_0$, centered at the null value $\theta_0$ with spread $SE_0$.

## 3. Deterministic PRNG & Worker Stream Architecture

All simulations in `js/prng.js` use a stateful **Mulberry32** generator seeded with an explicit 32-bit unsigned integer. Independent sub-streams (e.g., across parallel worker partitions or multi-study experiments) are derived deterministically via **SplitMix32** (`deriveStreamSeed(baseSeed, streamIndex)`), avoiding global state mutation and ensuring bit-for-bit reproducibility across runs of the same software version.
