Local-First Privacy & Security Architecture

        

          Zero External Transmission Guarantee: ResampleLab executes 100% locally inside your web browser using pure ECMAScript modules and High-DPI HTML5 Canvas. Uploaded CSV/TSV/JSON datasets are parsed strictly in local memory, never transmitted over the network, and never embedded in public telemetry. Exported CSV files automatically sanitize cells starting with =, +, -, or @ to prevent spreadsheet formula injection.
